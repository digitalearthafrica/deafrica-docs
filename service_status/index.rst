Overview
--------

This page provides a single source of truth for Digital Earth Africa service
disruptions, planned maintenance, resolved incidents, data-pipeline notices,
and software or package updates.

   **Platform status:** Operational

   **Last updated:** 15 July 2026

Current Alerts
--------------

.. container:: tech-alert tech-alert-warning

   **Sentinel-1, Sentinel-3, and Sentinel-5P Pipelines**

   :Status: Temporary disruption
   :Published: 15 July 2026
   :Expected restoration: End of July 2026
   :Affected services: Sentinel-1, Sentinel-3, and Sentinel-5P data pipelines

   Digital Earth Africa is implementing structural changes to the Sentinel-1,
   Sentinel-3, and Sentinel-5P data pipelines. These changes have temporarily
   interrupted the supply of new data.

   A data supply outage occurred in early July 2026. The affected pipelines are
   expected to resume operation by the end of July 2026. Missing observations
   will be backfilled after service restoration.

Why We Publish Tech Alerts
--------------------------

Tech Alerts help users understand changes that may affect data availability,
platforms, notebooks, APIs, and analytical tools. They allow users to plan
their work and avoid unexpected interruptions.
