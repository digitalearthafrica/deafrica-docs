.. _sandbox:

Analysis Sandbox
================

The Digital Earth Africa Analysis Sandbox is a cloud-based computational
platform that operates through a JupyterLab environment. It provides users
with access to Digital Earth Africa data and analytical tools, supporting
ad hoc reporting and the rapid development of new algorithms and workflows.

By creating a DE Africa Sandbox account or using the platform, you agree to
the :doc:`Sandbox Terms and Conditions <termsconditions>`.
